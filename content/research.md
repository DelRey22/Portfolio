## Research statement

Two or three paragraphs: the question you care about, why it is hard, and
what you are doing about it. Write it for a smart engineer outside your
subfield — a faculty member skimming for thirty seconds should come away
knowing what you do.

Be concrete. "I characterize switching loss in GaN half-bridges above
500 kHz, where existing datasheet models diverge from measurement by up to
30%" beats "I am interested in power electronics."
